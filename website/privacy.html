Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Prompt Memory ("we", "our", "the extension") is a browser extension that rewrites the prompt you have typed into an AI chat into a clearer one when you ask it to. This policy explains what data we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you control it.

💡 In short: nothing is sent until you press the ⊕ button (or its shortcut), and the first time you are told exactly what will be sent. Signed in, your draft and a few recent chat messages go to our server and an AI provider, and your drafts and rewrites are kept in your History. With your own API key and no account, the draft goes straight from your browser to that provider and never reaches us. Prompt tracking is off unless you turn it on. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Data We Collect

Account information

  • Email address, from Google sign-in. Used to identify your account and, only if you send feedback, to reply.

Prompts you ask us to rewrite (signed in)

  • Your draft: when you press ⊕ or use the keyboard shortcut, the text in the chat site's message box is sent to our server, which sends it to an AI provider to be rewritten. The draft and the rewrite are saved in your History, even if you dismiss the rewrite.
  • Conversation context: with the same request, up to six of the most recent visible messages from the open chat are sent, so the rewrite makes sense in context. This is on by default, happens only when you press ⊕, and is not kept as a profile. Turn it off in the Library: ⋯ → Privacy settings.
  • Whether you used a rewrite: when you put a rewrite into the chat box we record that, so we can tell how often rewrites are useful. Rewrites you use can also be remembered (as below) to shape your future rewrites.

Prompt tracking (off unless you turn it on)

  • When you turn it on, prompts you send to an AI chat in the normal way are logged so future rewrites can follow your style. With it off, nothing you send to a chat is logged by Prompt Memory. Turn it on or off in the Library: ⋯ → Privacy settings.

Saved prompts

  • Prompts you choose to save are stored on our server so you can insert them with // and use them as context for rewrites.
  • Text you select on a chat page and save with Save as context (for example, part of an answer) is stored the same way. Selecting text sends nothing; only pressing the button does. Turn the button off in the Library: ⋯ → Privacy settings.

Voice (signed in)

  • When you record a voice prompt, the audio is sent to our server for one transcription by Groq (Whisper). The audio is not stored. The transcript only becomes History if you then ask for it to be rewritten, like any other prompt.

Feedback

  • Bug reports and suggestions you send (Library: ⋯ → Send feedback) are stored with your email and basic browser information so we can follow up.
  • Ratings (thumbs up or down on a rewrite) are stored with that rewrite to improve quality.

Usage and diagnostics

  • For each rewrite we record when it happened, the style, which chat site, which AI model, how long it took, and whether it was used. These let us run the service and see whether it works. Our private dashboard shows only counts and averages, never prompt text.
  • When something fails (for example, the AI provider is unavailable) we record the kind of failure, the site and a one-way hash of your account id, with no prompt text. These records are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Each signed-in rewrite is also sent, with your draft, to an AI model that scores how much it improved the prompt. The score is saved with the rewrite in your History.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To rewrite your prompts, and to show you your History and saved prompts
  • To make your future rewrites fit you better, using your saved prompts, the rewrites you used, your ratings and (only if you turn it on) prompt tracking
  • To measure and improve rewrite quality and keep the service running
  • To answer bug reports and suggestions

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your browsing history.
  • We do not read any page except the five supported AI chat sites, and on those only the message box and recent messages when you press ⊕, text you select when you press Save as context, and, if you turned it on, prompts you send with prompt tracking.
  • We do not inject ads or track you across websites.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and Security

  • Our server runs on Hugging Face Spaces. Account data, History, saved prompts and feedback are stored in MongoDB Atlas; search vectors made from saved prompts and used rewrites are stored in Qdrant Cloud.
  • All traffic between the extension, our server and AI providers uses HTTPS.
  • Sign-in uses a token that expires after 7 days and is kept in your browser's extension storage.
  • Before your drafts and rewrites are stored in History, anything in them that looks like an API key, password or access token is masked.

5. Your Own API Key

You can use your own Groq, Gemini or OpenRouter API key instead of, or as well as, signing in. This changes where your text goes:

  • Your key is stored only on this device, in the extension's local storage. It is not placed in Chrome's synced storage, so it does not copy to your other browsers, and it is never written to our database.
  • With a key and no sign-in, your draft goes directly from your browser to the provider you chose. It never reaches our server, and nothing is logged or stored by us.
  • With a key and signed in, your draft passes through our server so your saved prompts and History work; your key is sent with that request, used for that one call, and not stored.
  • Provider policies differ. Groq states it does not train on API inputs. Google marks free-tier Gemini API data as used to improve its products. The extension shows this next to each provider.
  • Remove your key at any time with Remove key in the extension's settings.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google: sign-in (privacy policy); the Gemini API only if you choose it with your own key (terms)
  • Groq: rewrites, quality scores and voice transcription (privacy policy)
  • OpenRouter: only if you choose it with your own key
  • Hugging Face: hosts our server
  • MongoDB Atlas: database
  • Qdrant Cloud: search vectors

These services process data only to provide their part of Prompt Memory.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Turn off conversation context, or turn on and off prompt tracking, in the Library: ⋯ → Privacy settings.
  • Delete your data: in the extension's settings, Delete account and data erases your account, History, saved prompts, feedback, ratings and stored vectors from our servers. If a store is unavailable at that moment, we say the deletion is incomplete and keep your account so you can retry or contact us.
  • Retention: History and saved prompts are kept until you delete them or your account. Failure records are deleted after 90 days.
  • Sign out from the extension at any time. To ask what we hold about you, or for a copy, email us.

7a. Limited Use

Prompt Memory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use the data only to provide and improve the prompt-rewriting features described above.
  • We transfer it only to the service providers listed in section 6 to fulfil requests you made, and as required by law.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • No person at Prompt Memory reads your prompts, unless you send them to us yourself in feedback or ask us to look at a problem with your account.

8. Children

Prompt Memory is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what the extension sends or stores, we will say so in the extension before it applies.

10. Contact

Questions, or a request about your data: email hello.promptmemory@gmail.com, or use the Library: ⋯ → Send feedback.
