Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Prompt Memory ("we", "our", "the extension") is a browser extension that enhances your AI prompts. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can control it.

1. Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — collected via Google OAuth when you sign in. Used to identify your account and for optional follow-up on feedback you submit.

Prompt Data (with your control)

  • Prompts you enhance — when you explicitly click "Enhance" or use the keyboard shortcut, your prompt text is sent to our server for AI-powered enhancement. This is the core functionality of the extension.
  • Conversation context — when you enhance a prompt, the last few messages from the current chat page may be read to provide better context for enhancement. This only happens when you initiate an enhancement.
  • Passive prompt tracking — when enabled (off by default), prompts you submit on AI platforms are logged to improve future suggestions. You can disable this at any time via the ⚙ settings gear in the sidebar.

💡 Prompt Tracking is OFF unless you turn it on. With it off, prompts you submit normally to an AI chat are not logged by Prompt Memory. A prompt you explicitly enhance is different: signed-in enhancements send your draft and optional recent conversation context to our server and AI provider. We save the draft and rewrite in your enhancement history even if you dismiss the preview. With your own key and no sign-in, the draft goes directly to your chosen provider and we do not store it.

Conversation context reading is separate and is on by default: while fulfilling an enhancement you asked for, the extension reads the last few visible messages so the rewrite makes sense in context. It is sent with that single request and is not kept as a profile. Its own toggle sits alongside the first.

Voice Data

  • Audio recordings — when you use Voice-to-Prompt, microphone audio is sent to our server for one Whisper transcription request. Audio is not written to our database or retained. Transcription alone does not create prompt history. If you explicitly choose “Enhance transcript,” the corrected text is handled as a normal enhanced prompt under the Prompt Data and retention terms above. Aggregate voice telemetry contains no audio or transcript text.

Feedback

  • Bug reports and feature requests — when you submit feedback via the 💬 tab, your message, email, and browser info are stored to help us improve the product.
  • Prompt ratings — thumbs up/down on enhanced prompts help us improve enhancement quality.

Saved Prompts

  • Prompts you explicitly save — stored on our server so you can reuse them as context for future enhancements.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Enhance your prompts using AI
  • Improve enhancement quality based on your feedback and usage patterns
  • Provide your saved prompts and enhancement history
  • Respond to bug reports and feature requests

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect browsing history
  • We do not read any page content other than AI chat messages (and only when Prompt Learning is enabled or you click Enhance)
  • We do not inject ads or track you across websites
  • We do not sell your data to third parties

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored on secure servers (MongoDB Atlas)
  • Authentication uses JWT tokens with 7-day expiration
  • All API communication uses HTTPS encryption

5. Your Own API Key (BYOK)

You can add your own free API key from an AI provider in the extension's settings. This changes where your prompt text goes, so it is worth being precise about it.

  • Your key is stored only on your own device, in chrome.storage.local. It is never uploaded to us, never written to our database, and is deliberately not placed in Chrome's synced storage, so it does not replicate to your other browsers.
  • If you add a key but do not sign in, your prompt goes directly from your browser to the provider you chose. It never reaches our servers, and nothing is logged or stored anywhere by us.
  • If you add a key and also sign in, your prompt passes through our server so we can add your saved prompts and history as context. Your key is forwarded with that single request and used only to make that one call — we do not store it.
  • Provider data policies differ, and this matters. Groq states it does not train on API inputs. Google's Gemini pricing page marks free-tier data as "Used to improve our products: Yes" — so if you choose Gemini on its free tier, Google may use your prompts to improve their models. The extension shows this next to each provider so you can choose knowingly.
  • You can remove your key at any time with the "Clear" button in the extension settings.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth — for authentication (Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Groq API — for AI prompt enhancement and voice transcription (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Gemini API — only if you choose it as your own provider (API Terms)
  • OpenRouter — only if you choose it as your own provider
  • MongoDB Atlas — for data storage
  • Qdrant Cloud — for vectors derived from saved prompts and accepted enhancements, when cloud vector storage is configured

7. Your Rights & Controls

  • Prompt Learning is opt-in — it starts switched off. Turn it on or back off at any time in the ⚙ settings in the sidebar.
  • Delete your data — signed-in users can use “Delete account & data” in the extension popup to erase their account, prompt history, saved prompts, feedback, and stored embeddings. If a data store is unavailable, we report the deletion as incomplete and keep the account available so you can retry or contact us.
  • Retention — we keep your prompt history and saved prompts until you delete them. A successful account-deletion request removes your server-side account data.
  • Log out — sign out from the extension popup at any time

7a. Limited Use

Prompt Memory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use the data only to provide and improve the prompt-enhancement features described above.
  • We do not transfer this data to third parties except to the AI providers needed to fulfil a request you made, and as required by law.
  • We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • No human reads your prompts except where you have explicitly sent them to us in a bug report.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a data request: use the 💬 feedback tab in the extension, or email hello.promptmemory@gmail.com.
