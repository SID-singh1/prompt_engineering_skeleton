Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

Prompt Memory ("we", "our", "the extension") is a browser extension that enhances your AI prompts. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can control it.

1. Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — collected via Google OAuth when you sign in. Used to identify your account and for optional follow-up on feedback you submit.

Prompt Data (with your control)

  • Prompts you enhance — when you explicitly click "Enhance" or use the keyboard shortcut, your prompt text is sent to our server for AI-powered enhancement. This is the core functionality of the extension.
  • Conversation context — when you enhance a prompt, the last few messages from the current chat page may be read to provide better context for enhancement. This only happens when you initiate an enhancement.
  • Passive prompt tracking — when enabled (default), prompts you submit on AI platforms are logged to improve future suggestions. You can disable this at any time via the ⚙ settings gear in the sidebar.

💡 You are in control. Two separate toggles in the ⚙ sidebar settings let you independently disable prompt tracking and conversation context reading. When disabled, we only process data when you explicitly click "Enhance".

Voice Data

  • Audio recordings — when you use the voice-to-prompt feature, your microphone audio is sent to our server for transcription (via Whisper AI). Audio is processed in real-time and not stored permanently.

Feedback

  • Bug reports and feature requests — when you submit feedback via the 💬 tab, your message, email, and browser info are stored to help us improve the product.
  • Prompt ratings — thumbs up/down on enhanced prompts help us improve enhancement quality.

Saved Prompts

  • Prompts you explicitly save — stored on our server so you can reuse them as context for future enhancements.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Enhance your prompts using AI
  • Improve enhancement quality based on your feedback and usage patterns
  • Provide your saved prompts and enhancement history
  • Respond to bug reports and feature requests

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect browsing history
  • We do not read any page content other than AI chat messages (and only when Prompt Learning is enabled or you click Enhance)
  • We do not inject ads or track you across websites
  • We do not sell your data to third parties

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored on secure servers (MongoDB Atlas)
  • Authentication uses JWT tokens with 7-day expiration
  • All API communication uses HTTPS encryption

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth — for authentication (Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Groq API — for AI prompt enhancement and voice transcription
  • MongoDB Atlas — for data storage

6. Your Rights & Controls

  • Opt out of tracking — toggle off "Prompt Learning" in the ⚙ settings in the sidebar
  • Delete your data — contact us to request account and data deletion
  • Log out — sign out from the extension popup at any time

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the feedback form in the extension or email us.
