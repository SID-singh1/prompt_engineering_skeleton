Your prompts stay your choice

Prompt Memory improves a draft only when you ask. Here is what happens when you use it:

  • Enhance: Your draft goes to your chosen AI provider. If signed in, it goes through our server, is saved in your history, and up to six recent chat messages are sent for context by default. You can turn conversation context off in the sidebar.
  • Your own key: Without sign-in, your draft goes directly from this browser to the provider. If you also sign in, your key is forwarded through our server for each enhancement request so memory features can work.
  • Optional features: Google sign-in shares your email with us. Voice sends audio to our server and Groq for transcription when you record. Prompt Tracking, which logs submitted prompts, starts off and requires you to switch it on.
