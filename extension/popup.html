What gets sent, and when
  • Nothing leaves your browser until you press Improve. The first time, you are asked first.
  • Without an account, only the draft in the chat box is sent, to our server and an AI provider, and nothing is saved.
  • Signed in, the draft and up to six recent messages from the chat are sent, and the draft and rewrite are kept in your History. You can turn chat context off in the library.
  • With your own key and no account, the draft goes straight from this browser to that provider.
  • Signing in shares your Google email with us. Prompt tracking is off unless you turn it on.
