Better prompts, right where you chat

Write a rough draft in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or Grok. Prompt Memory turns it into a clearer prompt, and you decide whether to use it.

  1. 1
    Type as you normally wouldA quick, messy draft is fine.
  2. 2
    Press or Ctrl+Shift+EThe button sits at the bottom corner of the chat.
  3. 3
    Review, then replaceYour draft only changes if you choose to.
What gets sent, and when
  • Nothing leaves your browser until you press .
  • With your own key, the draft goes straight to that AI provider. It never touches our server.
  • Signed in, the draft goes through our server and is kept in your History, with a few recent messages from the chat for context. You can turn context off.
  • Prompt tracking is off unless you turn it on.
More detail
  • If you are signed in and also use your own key, the key is forwarded through our server with each rewrite so saved prompts and History keep working. It is never stored there.
  • Signing in with Google shares your email address with us.
  • Voice input sends the recording to our server and Groq to be transcribed, only when you record.
  • Conversation context sends up to six recent messages from the open chat with a signed-in rewrite. Turn it off in the Library: ⋯ → Privacy settings.
